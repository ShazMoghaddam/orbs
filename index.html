Let the orb move with sound. Bars follow the actual frequencies; other shapes pulse and push out on the beat.

The microphone is analysed in your browser only. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. In your own page, link an orb to a player with data-audio="#player"; it's added to your code when sound is on.

Desk display

Turn a spare iPad, tablet or monitor into an ambient orb. Open the link, tap for full screen, and it keeps the screen awake and drifts a few pixels each minute to protect it. On an iPad, add it to the home screen to hide the browser bars.
